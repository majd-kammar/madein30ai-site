About MadeIn30AI

MadeIn30AI is a creative initiative that highlights what can be achieved using artificial intelligence in just 30 minutes. Each project is conceptualized and executed with mobile-first tools and AI-driven workflows, demonstrating that powerful results are possible with minimal time and accessible technology.

From logo design to content creation and web development, MadeIn30AI combines speed, innovation, and creativity to deliver real-world solutions—all crafted with AI, often from a mobile device.
